Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Frido Ergonomic Assessment Service?

It's a personalised workspace evaluation done by a certified ergonomic advisor. You get a detailed report with product recommendations, posture fixes, and setup changes built around your body and work habits.

Is the assessment free?

The basic consultation is free. A detailed report with full recommendations may be chargeable — but if you purchase products from your report, the consultation fee gets redeemed.

Can I choose between a virtual or in-home visit?

Yes. You can book a virtual session at your convenience or schedule an advisor to visit your home or office directly.

Who conducts the assessment?

A trained physiotherapist with expertise in ergonomics — not a salesperson. Someone who understands your body, not just the products.

What does the report include?

Your report covers recommended products, posture corrections, workstation adjustments, and exercises — all specific to your setup and body.

Do you handle delivery and installation?

Yes. Once you purchase the recommended products, we take care of free delivery and installation. You don't have to figure anything out.
